The house

Twenty-two years on the same street.

Mehraab began in 2004 as a two-room workshop above a sari shop on 11th Main, embroidering for other labels. The first bridal commission under our own name went out in 2009.

Everything is still made within a kilometre of that first room. Zardozi, aari, dabka and gota patti are worked in-house; the kundan is hand-set by a family we have used for three generations. Nothing is outsourced to a market unit, which is the reason we can promise a date and keep it.

You will meet the person who draws your design, and again at every fitting. There are no salespeople here.

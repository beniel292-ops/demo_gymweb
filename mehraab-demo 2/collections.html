Collections

Four commissions, priced in bands.

A couture piece is not an SKU — the price moves with the embroidery density, the fabric and the number of panels. These are honest starting points, quoted properly at your first appointment.

Bridal · from ₹1,20,000 · 5–6 months

The wedding lehenga

We start with a drawing, not a fabric. Once the motif is agreed we choose the base — raw silk for weight and structure, organza where you want it to move — and the embroidery floor takes it for fourteen to twenty weeks.

  • — Hand-drawn design, revised with you before work begins
  • — Zardozi, dabka, aari and hand-set kundan
  • — Four fittings, the last in the week of the wedding
  • — Weighted dupatta, petticoat and blouse included
  • — Storage box and a lifetime of re-fittings

Groom · from ₹65,000 · 3–4 months

Sherwani & bandhgala

Most grooms come to us six weeks before the wedding and are surprised by the answer. Give us three months and you get a garment cut on your body, with a collar that sits flat in every photograph.

  • — Cut to measure, three fittings
  • — Hand-worked collar, placket and cuffs
  • — Matching safa, stole and brooch
  • — Our dresser ties the safa on the morning, at the venue

Occasion · from ₹48,000 · Trousseau · from ₹28,000

Reception, sangeet, and the rest of the trousseau

Sangeet outfits are built to be danced in — lighter linings, shorter trains, embroidery placed where it catches light rather than where it adds weight. For the trousseau we buy directly from weavers in Kanchipuram and Varanasi twice a year, and finish the borders and blouses here.

  • — Gowns, drape saris, sharara and lehenga sets
  • — Kanjivaram, Banarasi and Chanderi, sourced at the loom
  • — Blouse tailoring with two fittings
  • — Family coordination — mothers, sisters, and the groom's side in one palette
